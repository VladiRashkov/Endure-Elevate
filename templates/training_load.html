How this is calculated

Each run's cost is scored with TRIMP (Training Impulse) — duration weighted by how hard your heart worked, with harder effort counted disproportionately more than easy effort of the same length.

Acute load sums that score over the last 7 days. Chronic load is your last 28 days, averaged to a weekly rate. The ratio between them is ACWR — a sports-science measure of how your recent training compares to what your body has adapted to.
